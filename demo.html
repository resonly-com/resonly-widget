Manual Initialization Example

You can also initialize the widget manually via JavaScript:

window.ResonlyWidget.init({
  projectId: 'demo_project_123',
  host: 'https://api.example.com',
  mode: 'button',
  position: 'bottom-right',
  theme: 'light'
});
